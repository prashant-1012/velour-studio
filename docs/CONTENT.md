## Services Menu

### Hair

1. **Haircut & Styling** — A precision cut tailored to your face shape and lifestyle, finished with a professional blowout and styling. Includes a complimentary scalp massage. — *60 mins*
2. **Blowout & Finish** — Transform your hair with a salon-quality blowout using Dyson and ghd professional tools. Perfect for events or a mid-week refresh. — *30 mins*
3. **Keratin Treatment** — A deep-conditioning keratin smoothing treatment that eliminates frizz, adds mirror shine, and dramatically improves manageability for up to 3 months. — *150 mins*
4. **Hair Coloring** — Full head single-shade color using L'Oréal Professionnel or Schwarzkopf IGORA color systems. Includes a conditioning treatment and blowout. — *90 mins*
5. **Balayage** — Priya's signature hand-painted balayage technique for beautifully blended, sun-kissed color that grows out naturally. Consultation included. — *180 mins*
6. **Hair Spa** — A deeply nourishing treatment for damaged or over-processed hair. Includes steam therapy, protein mask, and a scalp pressure massage. — *60 mins*

### Skin

1. **Hydra Facial** — Our signature 6-step facial that cleanses, exfoliates, extracts, and infuses hyaluronic acid and antioxidant serums. Immediate, visible results. — *60 mins*
2. **Skin Cleanup** — A thorough deep-pore cleanse and mild peel that leaves skin fresh, clear, and balanced. Ideal for all skin types. — *45 mins*
3. **De-Tan Treatment** — A targeted tan-removal facial using natural enzyme peels and brightening serums. Visibly lightens sun damage after just one session. — *45 mins*
4. **Anti-Acne Treatment** — A medical-grade protocol combining salicylic acid exfoliation, blue-light therapy, and zinc-based soothing masks to target active acne and prevent future breakouts. — *60 mins*
5. **Gold Facial** — An indulgent 24-karat gold-infused ritual that boosts collagen, firms the skin, and leaves a luminous glow. A Velour Studio signature treatment. — *75 mins*

### Nails

1. **Gel Manicure** — Long-lasting gel polish in any shade from our curated collection. Includes shaping, cuticle care, and hand massage. — *45 mins*
2. **Gel Pedicure** — A thorough foot soak, scrub, and gel polish application with callus removal and a relaxing foot and calf massage. — *60 mins*
3. **Nail Art** — Intricate custom nail designs by Neha, from minimalist line work to detailed floral or geometric art. Consultation to discuss design included. — *60–90 mins*
4. **Nail Extensions (Acrylic / BIAB)** — Full set of acrylic or Builder-in-a-Bottle gel extensions, shaped and finished to your preference. Strong, natural-looking results. — *90 mins*

### Bridal

1. **Bridal Makeup** — A full bridal makeup look using airbrush foundation and premium products from Charlotte Tilbury, MAC, and NARS. Includes a trial session. — *180 mins*
2. **Pre-Bridal Package** — A curated 3-session package completed in the month before your wedding: hydra facial, body de-tan, gel manicure & pedicure, and hair spa. — *3 sessions*
3. **Engagement Look** — A polished, camera-ready makeup and hair look for engagement ceremonies and ring ceremonies. Lighter than full bridal, fully customisable. — *120 mins*

### Men's Grooming

1. **Haircut & Styling** — A precision cut from Rohan or Simran, tailored to your preferred length and style. Includes a hot towel refresh and styling with premium product. — *45 mins*
2. **Beard Styling & Shaping** — A detailed beard shaping, trim, and define service with hot towel treatment, beard oil conditioning, and edge clean-up. — *30 mins*
3. **Face Cleanup** — A men's-specific deep-cleansing facial with exfoliation, steam, and a brightening mask. Great for regular skin maintenance. — *45 mins*
4. **Head Massage** — A deeply relaxing scalp and neck pressure massage with warm Kama Ayurveda oil. The perfect add-on or standalone treatment. — *30 mins*
5. **D-Tan Treatment** — A full-face and neck tan-removal treatment formulated for men's skin, using natural enzymes and brightening peptides. — *45 mins*

## Studio Timeline

- **2015** — Velour Studio founded by Priya & Rohan Kapoor in a compact studio in Aundh, Pune.
- **2017** — Expanded services to include a full bridal suite; booked out for the full wedding season within 3 months.
- **2019** — Rohan certified in advanced skin treatments at the CIDESCO Academy, Bengaluru.
- **2021** — Relocated to a larger, purpose-designed studio at The Iris Arcade, Baner — with a dedicated men's grooming bay and nail lounge.
- **2023** — Crossed the milestone of 500+ happy clients with a 4.9★ average Google rating.
- **2025** — Launched the Velour Men's Grooming Lounge, with Rohan leading a team of certified skin and grooming specialists.
